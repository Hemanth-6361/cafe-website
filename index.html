About Code & Curry Café

Located inside the SSIT Campus, Code & Curry Café is the favorite hangout place for students and faculty. Whether you need a quick snack between classes, a refreshing coffee during project discussions, or a delicious meal after a long lab session, we have something for everyone.

We prepare fresh, hygienic, and tasty food every day with quality ingredients and excellent service.
